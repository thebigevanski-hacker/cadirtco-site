What We Do

Earthwork
Without
Boundaries.

California Dirt Co. brings together regional companies focused on the work that moves California forward. Our network spans core earthwork disciplines while keeping execution close to the market.

01

Excavation

Site excavation and earth removal for residential, commercial, agricultural, and development projects.

02

Grading

Rough grading, finish grading, site shaping, and earthwork preparation for construction.

03

Site Preparation

Preparing land and construction sites for the work that follows, from clearing through earthwork coordination.

04

Material Hauling

Dirt, aggregate, debris, and material movement coordinated through regional hauling networks.

05

Land Clearing

Vegetation, brush, surface material, and property preparation supporting development and land improvement.

06

Surface Removal

Concrete, asphalt, and other surface removal work supporting redevelopment, renovation, and site transformation.

Why Regional

California
Isn't One
Market.

California's earthwork markets are shaped by different terrain, development patterns, transportation corridors, soil conditions, contractor networks, and local requirements.

California Dirt Co. is built around that reality. Regional companies stay close to the work while the larger network creates the infrastructure to operate across markets.

01
Local Knowledge

Operators understand the market, relationships, conditions, and logistics where the work actually happens.

02
Shared Infrastructure

Technology, systems, standards, brand infrastructure, and resources can be developed once and strengthened across markets.

03
Network Scale

A regional footprint creates opportunities that are difficult to build from a single market alone.

04
One Standard

Different markets. Different operators. One California Dirt Co. standard for how the network represents itself.
